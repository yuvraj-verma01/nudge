This is now a strong product. The concept is coherent, the academic logic is actually embodied in the UX, and the prototype can communicate more than “here is a wellness app.”

But as a presentation prototype, I think it is still missing a few things that would make an evaluator immediately grasp the product instead of needing your explanation. The issue is no longer feature scope. It is storytelling.

The main standard I would use is:

> If I hand the phone to someone and say nothing, do they understand within 90 seconds what Mosaic knows, why it intervened, how it adapts, and why this is different from a tracker?

Right now, I think they would understand about 80–85% of it. I would make the following changes.

## 1. The biggest missing thing: make the “intelligence” visible earlier

The current flow shows data, then Body/Mind interpretation, then a recommendation. That's good.

But I want one very obvious causal sentence somewhere on Today:

> **Mosaic noticed:** little movement + a packed morning + 14 minutes free.

Then:

> **So right now:** a short, gentle movement break fits.

That connection is the product.

Do not make the evaluator mentally connect:

`2,180 steps + 86 min sitting + 3 meetings + 14 min free`

to

`Move for 2?`

Make Mosaic connect it for them.

This is probably the single most important UI improvement.

# 20. The product story should ultimately be this simple

I would judge every screen against these six questions:

### 1. What does Mosaic know?

Sleep, movement, schedule, mood, preferences.

### 2. What does Mosaic think could help?

Move or Recharge.

### 3. Why now?

There is a realistic opportunity.

### 4. What should I actually do?

One small action.

### 5. What if that doesn't fit?

Make it easier / swap / reject.

### 6. What did Mosaic learn?

The next intervention changes.

If every major flow reinforces those six things, you've got it.

## My biggest three changes before calling this final

If time is tight, prioritize these:

**First:** put `Mosaic noticed → therefore this action` directly on Today. Make the reasoning impossible to miss.

**Second:** visibly demonstrate adaptation later in the demo. Don't merely say “we'll remember”; actually change the next recommendation.

**Third:** put social support directly inside the hero intervention. Circle should help cause behaviour, not merely document it afterward.

Everything else is polish.

At this stage, I would not broaden Mosaic anymore. I would make those three ideas exceptionally clear, visually beautiful, and impossible for the evaluator to misunderstand.
